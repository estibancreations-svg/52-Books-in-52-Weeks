## Book Promise

A book about the questions that begin in childhood and keep unfolding through adulthood:

- Why do people do what they do?
- Why do certain conversations stay with us?
- Why do we recognize truth before we have the vocabulary to explain it?
- Why does curiosity pull us toward possibilities that later become reality?
- Why do some lessons only make sense after we have lived long enough to carry them?
- What are we supposed to do with what has been handed down to us?

The book uses true conversations, family memory, humor, emotional encounters, sayings, faith, and reflection to explore those questions.

## Anchor Story

A family friend visits the narrator's grandmother. The room is playful. There is teasing about a purse. Everyone laughs. The young narrator notices something.

**Hook:** *Everyone laughed. I did too. But I noticed something — and once I noticed it, I couldn't stop asking why.*

The chapter eventually connects to:

> When people show you who they are, believe them.

The line is not the chapter. The lived story that made the line understandable is the chapter.

## Book 01 Thematic Spine

1. **Curiosity** — the instinct to question what everyone else accepts.
2. **Recognition** — noticing what has not yet been explained.
3. **Identity** — learning that people often reveal themselves before we know how to interpret what we see.
4. **Discernment** — separating what people say from what their actions repeatedly show.
5. **Possibility** — curiosity becomes exploration; exploration becomes energy; energy can become reality.
6. **Purpose** — possibility still requires a reason.
7. **The Why** — what is this work for?
8. **Faith** — align the work with prayer and something larger than self.
9. **Stewardship** — build in a way that can bless others and responsibly steward the ship.
10. **Inheritance** — pass forward what was given to you.
