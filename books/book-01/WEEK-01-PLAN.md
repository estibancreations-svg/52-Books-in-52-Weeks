# Week 01 — The Why / True Stories

**Week begins:** 2026-10-05  
**Primary goal:** Build the Book 01 engine and finish one story deeply enough to prove the format.

## Week 1 Definition of Done

By the end of the week, Book 01 has:

- [ ] One locked paragraph explaining what the book gives the reader
- [ ] 7–12 true-story seeds
- [ ] A one-line lesson/question attached to every seed
- [ ] A truth/reconstruction label for every seed
- [ ] A provisional chapter order
- [ ] One fully drafted anchor chapter
- [ ] A one-page introduction or author's note
- [ ] A Week 2 drafting queue with the next 3 chapters
- [ ] A short list of people, photos, recordings, messages, or documents worth checking before publication

## Daily Run

### Day 1 — Why
Write the answer to:
**If I only finish one book this year, why does this one deserve to exist?**

Then write:
- Who is it for?
- What should they feel?
- What should they recognize?
- What should they carry away?

### Day 2 — Memory Harvest
Capture at least 7 story seeds without trying to polish them.

For each seed:
- person,
- place,
- approximate age/year,
- what was said,
- what happened,
- what you noticed,
- what you understand now,
- the possible lesson.

### Day 3 — The Grandmother Anchor
Build the family-friend / purse story as a full scene.

Do not rush to explain what was noticed. Let the room, teasing, laughter, body language, and young curiosity exist first.

End the first movement on the unanswered observation.

### Day 4 — Meaning
Write the adult reflection:
- What did younger you believe?
- What did you later understand?
- What did your grandmother understand that you did not?
- Where does “When people show you who they are, believe them” belong?
- What is the difference between identity, behavior, assumption, and discernment in this memory?

### Day 5 — The Why / Faith / Stewardship
Draft the connective essay or chapter that moves from curiosity to possibility to purpose:

**I question → I explore → I put energy toward possibility → possibility can become reality → but why should it become reality?**

Then connect that question to prayer, blessing beyond self, and stewardship.

### Day 6 — Architecture
Arrange the story seeds into a provisional Book 01 map.

Recommended movement:
1. Curiosity
2. What I Noticed
3. What They Were Trying to Tell Me
4. People Show You
5. Possibility
6. The Things I Chased
7. The Things That Chased Me
8. The Why
9. Prayer
10. Stewardship
11. What We Inherit
12. What We Pass Forward

This is a working sequence, not a locked table of contents.

### Day 7 — Proof
Read the anchor chapter aloud.

Mark:
- where the story drags,
- where you explain too much,
- where the younger voice sounds too adult,
- where a detail feels invented,
- where the lesson lands naturally,
- and where the reader would want the next chapter.

Then create the Week 2 queue.

## Week 1 Rule

**Do not chase 52 books this week. Build the machine that can make Book 1 honestly and repeatedly.**
